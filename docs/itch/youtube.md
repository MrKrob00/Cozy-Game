## Description

Mow an overgrown village back to life, one cozy stripe at a time. 🌱

The grass has won: the yard, Grandma's garden, the village square and the old orchard are all lost under waist-high grass. Grab your mower, push it stripe by stripe, and watch colour, birdsong and neighbours return to every garden.

🌿 Satisfying light-and-dark stripes on every pass
🦔 Sleepy hedgehogs, frog choirs and lost things hidden in the grass
🔧 Upgrade your mower to tame the purple weeds
🎈 Little surprises: a four-leaf clover, a hot-air balloon with a gift, a sun shower
🎉 Bring the whole village back and end the day with a festival

No timers, no enemies, no way to lose. Just you, a mower and a summer afternoon.
