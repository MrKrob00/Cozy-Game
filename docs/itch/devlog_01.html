Hi, and welcome to the very first devlog of Cozy Grass Cut!

[image: 1_overgrown_yard.jpg]

What is it?

A small, relaxing game about mowing. You arrive in a little village where the grass has won: the yard, Grandma's garden, the village square and the old orchard are all lost under waist-high grass. You push your mower, stripe by stripe, and the village slowly comes back to life: colours warm up, birds start singing, neighbours return home, and in the end there's a festival.

No timers, no enemies, no way to lose. The goal was simple: mowing should feel good on its own, even with nothing to do.

[image: 2_stripes.jpg]

What's in the first version

  • Four gardens, each with its own look: the yard, Grandma's garden, the village square and the fruit orchard.
  • Things hidden in the grass: stone paths, benches, a fountain, sleepy hedgehogs and frogs, and lost things to return to their owners through the Lost & Found.
  • A workshop to upgrade the mower: a sharper blade (you'll need it for the purple weeds), a bigger bag, a faster motor.
  • Little surprises while you mow: a four-leaf clover, a hot-air balloon dropping a gift, a rabbit with a carrot, a frog chorus by the pond, a sun shower that leaves the grass sparkling with dew.
  • Sleep at your front door: a new day begins and the grass grows back a little.
  • Keyboard, gamepad and touch controls, and 10 languages.

[image: 6_orchard.jpg]

A few secrets of the grass

  • Every garden keeps a "mow map": a grid of cells about 12 cm wide. It draws the grass, counts the percentage and fills your bag, all from the same data.
  • The light and dark stripes depend on which way you push the mower, just like on a football pitch. Mow over a stripe the other way and it changes colour, so you can redo the pattern as often as you like.
  • No pixel hunting: thin strips left between your passes are trimmed by themselves, and near the end fireflies gather over the last tufts so you can find them.
  • The mower is silent on purpose. You hear the grass, the birds and the music, not an engine.
  • The game runs on a budget phone: the grass is drawn in 4×4 m pieces, and the whole village takes about 75 draw calls.
