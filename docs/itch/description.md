## Short description or tagline

Mow an overgrown village back to life, one cozy stripe at a time.

## Описание (Description)

**Cozy Grass Cut** is a relaxing mowing game about a little village lost under waist-high grass. Push your mower stripe by stripe, uncover hidden paths, lost treasures and sleepy hedgehogs, and watch colour, birdsong and neighbours return to every garden.

The grass has won. The yard, Grandma's garden, the village square and the old orchard have all disappeared under it. Grab the mower and bring them back.

- **Satisfying mowing.** Every pass leaves a neat light-and-dark stripe, clippings fly, the bag fills up and goes into the compost bin.
- **Four gardens to restore**: the yard, Grandma's garden, the village square and the fruit orchard, each with its own look and secrets.
- **Hidden in the grass**: stone paths, benches, a fountain, and lost things to return to their owners: Grandma's glasses, a garden gnome, a rubber duck, a message in a bottle…
- **Little surprises**: a four-leaf clover, a hot-air balloon with a gift, a rabbit with a carrot, a frog chorus by the pond.
- **Upgrade your mower**: a sharper blade for the tough weeds, a bigger bag, a faster motor.
- **A village that comes alive**: colours warm up, birds sing, neighbours come home, and it all ends with a festival.

No timers, no enemies, no way to lose. Just you, a mower and a summer afternoon.

Play on PC, on your phone or right in the browser, with keyboard, gamepad or touch. In 10 languages. About an hour to bring the whole village back, and you can keep mowing after that.
